Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Tic-Tac-Toe Pro ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal data and upholding high standards of data security, transparency, and user privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, and process your information when you access or use our web platform.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to provide and secure our gaming and rewards service:

  • Account Authentication: Email address, player display name, and avatar URL provided when signing in via Google OAuth, 6-Digit Email OTP, or password.
  • Gameplay & Statistics: Match scores, games played, wins, losses, draws, and accumulated points balance.
  • Reward Redemptions: Record of redemption requests (date, reward tier, points deducted, fulfillment status).
  • Local Preferences: Audio sound toggle, dark mode preferences, and difficulty selection saved in your browser's Local Storage.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Maintain and synchronize your game points and account progress across devices.
  • Process and verify Google Play gift card redemptions.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, automated bots, and anti-cheat violations.
  • Respond to customer support inquiries and provide technical assistance.

4. Data Security & Storage

Our database is hosted with Supabase PostgreSQL, secured by Row Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring users can only read and modify their own authorized records. All web traffic is encrypted via HTTPS / TLS 1.3.

5. No Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or email addresses to any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You retain full rights to request the deletion of your account and associated game statistics. Contact our support team at support@tictactoepro.app to request complete data erasure.
